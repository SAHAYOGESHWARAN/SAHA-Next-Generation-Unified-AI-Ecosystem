## 🎮 How to Use (Step by Step)

### **Eye Tracking**
1. Click **"👁️ Eyes On"** at top
2. Move mouse anywhere
3. Watch eyes follow in real-time
4. See natural blinking

### **Voice Commands**
1. Click **"🎤 Voice On"** at top
2. Click **microphone button** (bottom-right, cyan circle)
3. Say clearly: **"Open dashboard"**
4. Wait for AI response (spoken!)
5. Change language with 🌐 button

### **AI Chat**
1. Click **"💬 Chat AI"** at top
2. Chat window opens (bottom-left)
3. Type question or ask anything
4. AI responds intelligently
5. Click close to minimize

### **Hologram Effects**
1. Click **"🌐 Hologram"** at top
2. See particles animate background
3. Watch connecting lines
4. Toggle on/off

### **Navigation**
- Click **"Enter Dashboard"** button
- Click **"Explore Tools"** button
- Use voice commands to navigate
- All pages fully functional
